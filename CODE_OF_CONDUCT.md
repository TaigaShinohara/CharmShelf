# Code of conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Japanese and English are both welcome.

In issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces:

- Be considerate and discuss the work with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect differing views and accept corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or make personal attacks on others.
- Do not share another person's private information, media, or credentials without permission.
- Keep sexualized comments, unwanted sexual attention, and other disruptive conduct out of project discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions, limit participation, or block accounts to protect contributors. Decisions should reflect the conduct and its effect on the project.

To report a concern privately, use the contact listed in the [privacy policy](docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md). Include only the information needed to understand the incident. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
